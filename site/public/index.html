Meeting transcriber for Linux, macOS and Windows

Meeting transcripts, word for word.

Puplify listens to your microphone and to the call's audio, writes down what is said as it's said, and saves it as plain text. No bot joins the meeting. No summary rewrites it.

Who said what

Two sources, two tracks.

Puplify never guesses who is talking. It listens in two places and labels each line by where the sound came from.

Microphone
Me. Everything you say, as you say it. Wear headphones, or your mic also hears the other side and their words show up twice.
System audio
Them. Whatever the call plays: Zoom, Meet, Teams, a browser tab. Overlapping speech stays on its own track, so cross-talk doesn't get tangled.
Labels
Rename Me and Them in Settings. Saved transcripts use your names.
Output
Timestamp, speaker, words. Saved as .txt, .md or .json. No summary, no action items, nothing rewritten.
Bots
None. Nothing joins the call. Puplify is an app on your computer listening to your computer.
Calendar
Connect Google or Outlook and Puplify starts recording when you join a meeting from your calendar, then stops when you leave the call. Or have it ask first.

When the meeting ends

Sent where you already keep things.

Stop recording and the transcript goes to every destination you've switched on. It's autosaved while you record, and any stored transcript can be re-sent later.

  • OneDrivePersonal and work or school accounts.
  • Google DriveA folder in My Drive.
  • DropboxAny Dropbox folder.
  • NotionOne page per transcript.
  • A local folderA synced folder, or an Obsidian vault.
  • Any webhookZapier, n8n, Make, your own server.

Webhook events

recording.started     a recording begins
transcript.segment    each line, live
recording.completed   the full text

JSON over POST, HMAC-signed

Command line

No window required.

The same engine as a single binary. Record from a terminal, transcribe a WAV file, script the rest.

$ puplify record --title "Weekly sync"
$ puplify transcribe standup.wav
$ puplify list
$ puplify show <id> --format md
$ puplify deliver <id>

Before you start

macOS. Apple Silicon only (Intel Macs: build from source). The quickest install is one Terminal command: it downloads the latest release and puts Puplify in Applications, ready to open.

curl -fsSL https://puplify.com/install.sh | sh

Downloaded the .dmg instead? The build isn't notarized yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Puplify.app once, or choose Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

The first recording asks for Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording access. Allow both, then restart Puplify.

Linux. Needs PipeWire (with pipewire-pulse) or PulseAudio.
